Match-Cut Editing Tool

To explore the practical use of the proposed method, we developed a prototype editing tool. Given a set of images and the pairwise similarities computed by our method, We can create match cuts without manually comparing every pair of images.

Methodology

Our Model

A segmentation encoder (SAM or SAM2) were used as the feature extraction backbone, since capturing the spatial characteristics needed for match cut retrieval. A Spatial Compression Module(SCM) reduces the feature map to a 1024-d vector, and a projection head(PH) is added only for contrastive learning. At inference, the PH is discarded and candidates are retrieved by cosine similarity.

Synthetic Match Cut Image Dataset

We constructed a rendering-based synthetic dataset by varying shot size, camera settings, character, pose, and background to address the scarcity of real-world match-cut data and concerns related to copyright and privacy. Images sharing the same shot size, camera variant, and pose are defined as belonging to the same match cut type.

Training Strategy

By adapting the training strategy of SimCLR, we encourage the embeddings of two synthetic images from the same match cut type to become closer, while pushing apart embeddings of images from different types within a batch of 2N images. Through this process, the parameters of the SCM and PH are optimized to learn representations suitable for match cut image retrieval.

Evaluations

We compare our method with the existing method, MatchCutting . Our method demonstrates better performance in both the quantitative evaluation and user study.
